Cancellations

Last updated 8 October 2026

The Picture-Line unlock is a one-time digital purchase. Your licence is delivered on screen the moment you pay, so all sales are final.

Try it first

The free version is the full app with up to 5 photos. Use it for as long as you like before you decide to unlock.

Nothing to cancel

There is no subscription and nothing renews, so there is no order to cancel after you pay.

Charged twice by mistake?

If a payment error charged you more than once for the same unlock, email kjrlabs9@gmail.com with both payment IDs (they start with pay_). The extra payment goes back to the card, UPI account or bank account you paid from.

Something not working?

Write to kjrlabs9@gmail.com and I'll help you get it working.
