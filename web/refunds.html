Refunds

Last updated 8 October 2026

If the unlock isn't right for you, you can have your money back within 7 days of buying. No questions asked.

How to ask

  1. Email kjrlabs9@gmail.com within 7 days of your purchase.
  2. Include your payment ID (it starts with pay_) or the email address you paid with.

I'll reply within 2 working days and start the refund through Razorpay. The money goes back to the card, UPI account or bank account you paid from. Razorpay and your bank usually take 5 to 7 working days to show it.

After a refund

The licence key from a refunded purchase may stop working in a later version of the app. The free version keeps working as before.

Cancellations

The unlock is a one-time digital purchase, delivered instantly, so there's no order to cancel and nothing renews. If you were charged twice by mistake, write and I'll refund the extra payment straight away, even after 7 days.
