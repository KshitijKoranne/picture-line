Delivery

Last updated 8 October 2026

Picture-Line is a digital product. Nothing is shipped, and there's no physical item.

The app

The app is a download from this website, Picture-Line-1.0.dmg, available immediately to anyone, worldwide.

The unlock

  • As soon as Dodo Payments confirms your payment, you're taken to a thank-you page showing your licence key, with a button that opens Picture-Line and activates it in one click.
  • The page also has a button to email the key to yourself, so you have a copy.
  • If you close the page before saving the key, get it again any time on the find my key page using your payment ID.

Delivery is instant in almost every case. If a payment is still being confirmed, the page will say so; give it a minute and refresh. If you don't receive your key within an hour of paying, write to kjrlabs9@gmail.com and I'll sort it out the same day where possible.
