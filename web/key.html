Picture-Line / Find my licence key

Lost your key?

Enter your payment ID and you'll get the same licence key again, ready to paste into Picture-Line.

It starts with pay_ followed by 14 letters and numbers. You'll find it in the receipt email Razorpay sent when you paid, and on the thank-you page.

Activating the key

  1. Open Picture-Line's Settings and go to About.
  2. Click Unlock…, then Have a licence key?
  3. Paste the key and click Activate.

Can't find the payment ID? Write to kjrlabs9@gmail.com with the email address you paid with, and I'll send your key.
