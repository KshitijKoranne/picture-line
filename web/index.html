A washing line for your photos.

Most of the photos we love live in an album we rarely open. Picture-Line puts a handful of them where you'll actually see them: on your Mac desktop, clipped to a length of twine like prints drying on a line.

It's a small, quiet thing. Drag in the faces you miss, write a word or two underneath, and get on with your day. When you glance at the desktop, they're there.

Questions people ask.

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Picture-Line works entirely on your Mac. Your photos, captions and notes stay on your Mac, there's no account to make, and the app doesn't send analytics or talk to any server.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon (M1 and later) running macOS 14 Sonoma or newer. Intel Macs aren't supported.

Will it get in the way while I work?

It hangs on the desktop, behind every window, so your apps stay exactly where they are. Hold ⌃ ⌥ to bring the string forward for a moment, or choose Keep String in Front of Windows from its menu bar icon.

What do I get for free, and what does the unlock add?

Free: up to 5 photos, fairy lights at sunset, the idle breeze, nails for extra loops, captions and notes. The $4.99 unlock (₹399 in India) adds up to 12 photos, Diwali, Holi and Christmas decorations, birthday glow and confetti, string and clip colours, frames and five handwritings.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and the unlock is yours. There's no account and nothing renews.

How do I pay from India?

Choose ₹399 with UPI or card. Checkout is handled by Razorpay and supports UPI, cards and netbanking. Your licence key appears on screen the moment the payment goes through.

Why does it ask for my location?

Only to work out when the sun sets where you are, so the fairy lights come on at dusk. It stays on your Mac. You can say no and switch the lights to always on, or off, in Settings.

Does it slow my Mac down?

When nothing on the string is moving, Picture-Line stops drawing altogether. It only wakes up when your pointer comes near, the breeze starts, or the lights twinkle.
