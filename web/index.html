For the people who aren't in the room.

Your son in a hostel two states away. Your parents in another time zone. The dog who still waits at the gate. Most of us carry thousands of photos and look at almost none of them. Picture-Line puts a few back where your eyes already are.

The drawing on the fridge

Mira drew this the week you moved to Pune for work. On the back she wrote that the rocket is so Papa can come home faster. It used to be on the fridge. Now it's the first thing you see every morning, before the inbox.

Two cups at five

Your father still makes two cups of chai at five o'clock, out of habit. You're nine hundred kilometres away. So the cup hangs on your desktop, and at five you make yours too.

Still waiting at the gate

Ma sends a photo of Bruno every Sunday. He still sits by the gate in the evenings, listening for your scooter. Double-click his photo and the back says so, in your own words.

The trip the group chat won't forget

Six friends, one green car, no air conditioning. You live in four cities now. The photo swings a little when your pointer passes, and for a second it's that road again.

It feels like paper and twine.

The string sways and the photos swing. Leave your Mac for a minute and a soft breeze moves them.

Press and hold to look closer
The photo lifts off the string and fills your screen. Let go, and it clips back.
Pull the string and tap ⌥ twice
A nail goes in where you're holding it. Two loops, three loops, however your wall looks at home.
Hold ⌃ ⌥ to peek
The string sits behind your windows. Hold the keys and it comes forward until you let go.

Fairy lights at sunset. Diyas at Diwali.

The lights come on by themselves when the sun goes down where you live. On festival days the string dresses up: diyas for Diwali, colour for Holi, and at Christmas stars, red baubles and candy canes, with snowflakes that settle on the string and fall off when you shake it. On a birthday, that person's photo glows and a little confetti falls.

Lighter than the photo frame on your desk.

It stays open all day, so it was built to stay out of the way. When nothing is moving, Picture-Line stops drawing altogether. It only wakes when your pointer comes near, the breeze blows or the lights twinkle.

Questions

Do my photos get uploaded anywhere?

No. Picture-Line works entirely on your Mac. Your photos, captions and notes stay on your Mac. There is no account, and the app sends nothing anywhere.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon (M1 and later) running macOS 14 Sonoma or newer.

Will it get in the way while I work?

No. It hangs on the desktop, behind every window. Hold ⌃ ⌥ to bring it forward for a moment, or choose Find My String from its menu bar icon.

Will it slow my Mac down?

No. When the string is still it uses 0% of the processor and about 60 MB of memory.

How does the unlock work?

Click Unlock in the app or on this page and pay once. The thank-you page has an Open in Picture-Line button that unlocks the app straight away. You also get a licence key, for a second Mac or a fresh install.
